Breathing practice at your desk

You don't need to lie down or close your eyes for slow breathing. It works sitting at a desk, and done through your nose nobody nearby will notice.

Set up in ten seconds

  • Feet flat on the floor and your back against the chair.
  • Hands resting on your thighs or the desk.
  • Unclench your jaw and let your shoulders drop away from your ears.
  • Look away from the screen, or lower your eyes to the desk.

One minute

Six breaths: in through your nose for 4, out through your nose for 6. That's the whole thing. It fits while a file uploads or a page loads.

Five minutes

Turn your screen off or step away from it. Breathe at the same pace and count each out-breath up to ten, then start again at one. Three rounds of ten is five minutes. The free breathing timer keeps the pace for you if you'd rather not count.

Breathing nobody hears

Keep it through your nose, keep each breath small, and let your belly move rather than your shoulders. Slow breathing doesn't need to be deep. Small, slow breaths make less sound and are more comfortable to keep up at a desk.

Fitting it into a working day

  • When a call ends, before you open the next thing.
  • Before you open your inbox in the morning.
  • When you notice you've been holding your breath while you type. Many people do.
  • With a recurring reminder in your calendar at a low point of the afternoon.
