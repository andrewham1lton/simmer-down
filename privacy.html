Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

In short: Simmer Down doesn't collect any of your data. There's no account, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or SDKs. Your readings and your sessions stay on your iPhone.

The camera

During a session, Simmer Down turns on the torch and reads the rear camera's picture of your fingertip live, on your iPhone, to see your pulse. The picture is never saved, never recorded and never sent anywhere. Simmer Down doesn't use your photo library or your microphone.

What stays on your device

When you finish a session, Simmer Down keeps a short summary of it: the date, how long you breathed, your pace, your heart-rate swing, your sync score, your best stretch in sync, the share of clean beats, and how you said you felt before and after, if you tapped one. It keeps no beat-by-beat data and no pictures. The summaries are stored only on your iPhone, in a file that can't be read while your iPhone is locked. They are included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer, if you use one), so a new iPhone restored from it keeps your history; they are never synced or sent to us. Your preferences, such as your usual pace and session length, are stored on your iPhone too.

You can delete every session at any time in Simmer Down, under Settings › Delete all history, or one at a time in History. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, Simmer Down schedules it on your iPhone as a local notification. If you start a free trial with notifications allowed, it also schedules one reminder two days before the trial ends. These never include a reading, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else to deliver them. You can turn them off in Simmer Down's Settings or in iOS Settings.

No account, no servers

Simmer Down has no account to sign in to and no servers of its own. It doesn't use iCloud sync, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Purchases

Simmer Down's subscription is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple gives developers anonymous sales totals.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They help fix bugs and don't identify you. We receive these only through Apple. Apple's opt-in App Analytics works the same way: Apple may share anonymous usage totals with us only if you've chosen to share with app developers. You control both at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

How long we keep things

  • Everything on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Who else is involved

Apple handles payments and, only if you opt in, App Analytics and crash reports, under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Children

Simmer Down is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
