Simmer Down

Slow, paced breathing for relaxation, on iPhone. Rest a fingertip over the rear camera, follow the breathing guide at about six breaths a minute, and watch your heart rate rise and fall with each breath.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your readings stay on your iPhone.

Simmer Down is for relaxation and breathing practice. It is not a medical device.
