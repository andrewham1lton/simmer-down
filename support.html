Where does my fingertip go?

Rest the pad of one fingertip lightly over the rear camera lens nearest the torch, covering both the lens and the light. Rest your hand on something so it stays still. Press gently: pressing hard squeezes out the light the app reads.

No number appears

That's on purpose for the first few seconds. Simmer Down waits until a few beats in a row agree before it shows anything. If it keeps waiting, let your hand relax, rest your index finger softly over the cameras, and warm your hand if it's cold.

The reading keeps dropping out

Movement and light leaking in around your fingertip are the usual causes. Sit comfortably, rest your arm, and keep the same light touch. Lifting your fingertip briefly pauses the session; rest it again to carry on.

The camera area feels warm

The torch stays on during a session, so the back of the iPhone warms a little. That's normal. Take a break between sessions if it feels too warm.

Changing the pace or length

Set the pace and length every session starts with in Settings › New sessions. You can still change both before you begin.

Subscriptions and refunds

Cancel: in Simmer Down, go to Settings, then Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Not showing as subscribed: in Simmer Down, go to Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Refunds: purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, choose "I'd like to", then "Request a refund".

Privacy

Simmer Down collects no data: no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The camera picture is read live and never saved, and your session summaries stay on your iPhone. Read our privacy policy.

About your wellbeing

Simmer Down is for relaxation and breathing practice. It is not a medical device. Breathe gently and comfortably; if you feel light-headed or uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally. If you have questions about your health, talk to your doctor.
