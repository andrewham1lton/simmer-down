A two-minute breathing reset

Two minutes is about twelve slow breaths. It's short enough to fit between one thing and the next, and long enough to settle into a slow rhythm before you carry on.

The two minutes

  1. Put down what you're holding. Set a timer for two minutes, or open the free breathing timer and choose 2 minutes.
  2. Sit or stand with your feet on the floor and let your shoulders drop.
  3. Start with one slow breath out, longer than usual.
  4. Breathe in through your nose for a count of 4.
  5. Breathe out gently for a count of 6, through your nose or softly pursed lips.
  6. Repeat until the timer ends. Twelve breaths at this pace is two minutes.
  7. Take one normal breath and notice how you feel before you go back to what you were doing.

Where it fits

  • Before a call or meeting you'd rather not have.
  • After reading a difficult message, before you reply.
  • Between two tasks, when your attention is still on the last one.
  • In the car once you've parked, before you go inside.
  • In a queue or a waiting room, with your eyes open.

If counting is hard

Shorten it to 3 in and 5 out. Or skip the seconds and count breaths instead, one finger for each out-breath until you've used both hands. Some people find it easier to follow something that moves, such as a pacer on a screen.

Making it a habit

Tie it to something that already happens every day, such as closing your laptop at lunch or switching off the car. For a longer practice at the end of the day, see the evening wind-down routine.
